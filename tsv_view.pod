=head1 NAME

tsv_view -- tiny utility for viewing TSV files

=head1 SYNOPSIS

B<tsv_view> I<[options]> I<[files...]>

=head1 OPTIONS

=over 6

=item B<-h> or B<--help>

Display this screen.

=item B<-l>

Replace \n with LF.

=item B<-H>

Consider first line as a header.

=back
